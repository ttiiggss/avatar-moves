Core Mechanics

The Flow System & Beyond

Four buttons. One Flow key. Infinite expression. Understand these systems to compete.

Pillars

Four Attack Buttons

Light, Medium, Heavy, and Flow. Chain L→M→H for basic combos. Each button has unique standing, crouching, and aerial versions per character.

L — Fast checks, confirms
M — Balanced reach and speed
H — Slower, high-reward starters
F — Movement, cancels, extensions
Signature Mechanic

The Flow System

Flow is the connective tissue of the game — not just a fourth attack. It governs movement, cancels, combo extensions, and defensive escape.

Flow Cancel — Cancel normal recovery into movement
Flow Escape — Direction+F to slip through pressure
Flow Stance — Hold F to auto-parry (costs 62.5 Chi)
Unbalanced — Empty gauge = no Flow, all hits are Counter Hits
Resources

Energy (Chakra) System

Build Energy through special hits, throws, overheads, and Flow Stance. Spend it on enhanced specials or devastating supers.

Max Energy: 5 (round 1) → 7 (after losing round)
EX Specials: 1-2 Energy
Super Technique: 4 Energy (Hard Knockdown)
Final Technique: 7 Energy (Invulnerable, max power)
Combat

Counter Hits & Throws

Hit during an opponent's startup, active, or recovery frames for a Counter Hit. Flow Stance is vulnerable to throws — a Critical Throw deals massive Chi damage.

Counter Hit: +6 frames additional hitstun
Critical Throw: 125 Chi damage (187.5 vs Flow Stance)
Flow Stance: Generates Chakra after 60 idle frames
Guard Reversal: H+F while guarding (invulnerable)
Strategy

Support Characters

Before each match, choose one of three supports. They provide persistent buffs, cover weaknesses, extend combos, and can even transform your character's playstyle.

3 supports per fighter = 3 variations to learn
Some can lock/unlock moves (Ozai)
Some change stances entirely (Azula's Firelord Madness)
Zaheer gets on-screen tag assists (unique)
Language

Numpad Notation

Directions are written as numbers on a keyboard numpad while facing right. All combos in this guide use this universal fighting game notation.

236L = ↓↘→ + Light (quarter-circle forward)
214H = ↓↙← + Heavy (quarter-circle back)
6F = → + Flow (forward Flow move)
j.M = jumping Medium (airborne)
